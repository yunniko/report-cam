Report Cam — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Report Cam is a camera app that stamps a photo with the date, the time, the location and a text of your choice, and hands the photo to a messenger or another app you pick. It is published by Julienika (julienika.cz). This policy explains what the app does with data on your phone.

The short version

  • Report Cam has no internet access of its own. It cannot send anything anywhere by itself.
  • Nothing is collected by the publisher: no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reports.
  • Your photos and your location stay on your phone until you choose to send a photo.

Camera

The app uses the camera only while its live preview is on screen, to take the photo you ask for. No photo is taken without your tap.

Location

With your permission, the app reads your location while it is open, to print it on the stamp and to store it in the photo's metadata (EXIF and XMP), so that the place is recorded in the photo you send. Location is read only while the app is visible; there is no background location. You can turn the location line off in Settings, or deny the permission, and the photo is then stamped without a location.

Place name

If you turn on "Place name" in Settings (off by default), the app asks the phone's geocoding service for the street address of the photo's location. On most phones this service is provided by Google and sends the coordinates to Google's servers under Google's own privacy policy. With "Place name" off, no location leaves the phone through the app.

Photos and texts

A photo waiting to be sent, with its note and message, is kept in the app's private storage on your phone. It is removed when you send it, retake it, or when the time limit set in Settings passes. If "Save to gallery when sent" or "Save discarded photo" is on, a copy goes to the Pictures/Report Cam folder of your phone, where other apps with gallery access can see it.

Sending

When you tap a send button or Share, the photo and the message are handed to the app you chose (for example WhatsApp or Telegram). From that moment the photo is in that app's hands and under that app's privacy policy. Report Cam does not see what happens to it afterwards.

Permissions

  • Camera: to take the photo.
  • Location (precise and approximate): to stamp the photo with the place; optional.
  • Photos and media (Android 9 and older only): to save a copy to the gallery.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes

A changed policy is published at the same address with a new "Last updated" date; the app shows the policy it was built with.
